        Set up the “Find videos” bookmark

        A website can't look inside other websites, so CastPilot uses a Safari bookmark that scans the page you're on.

        1. Tap Copy bookmark code above.
        2. In Safari, open any page, tap Share then Add Bookmark. Name it Find videos and save.
        3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap Find videos, delete its address and paste the copied code. Tap Done.
        4. On a page with a video: start the video, tap the address bar, type Find videos and tap the bookmark. Its videos open here.

        Found videos open in a Safari tab. If you also added CastPilot to your Home Screen, that copy keeps its own separate Up Next and history.

              What this device can do

              iPhone and iPad (Safari): AirPlay to Apple TV and AirPlay TVs. Many Samsung, LG, Sony, Vizio and Roku TVs support AirPlay.
              Android or a computer (Chrome): Chromecast and Google TV.
              Smart TVs over DLNA, and Roku's own casting, need the CastPilot iPhone app. Browsers aren't allowed to search your network for them.

              Good to know

              Some websites only let their own pages play their videos. Those can play here, but a TV may refuse them. Videos with DRM (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video…) can't be cast by any third-party app.

              Everything you save here (history, Up Next, favourites) stays in this browser on this device.
